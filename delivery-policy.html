Digital Delivery Policy

How order confirmation, delivery timing, customer communication, and delivery issues should be handled.

This is structured sample policy content and requires legal and operational approval before launch.
OverviewDelivery timingDelivery methodCustomer responsibilitiesDelivery issues

1. Overview

LinkPVA products are intended for digital delivery after payment and order confirmation. The exact method, included information, estimated timeframe, and any customer requirements must be stated on the relevant product page.

2. Delivery timing

Delivery estimates begin after an order is successfully confirmed and any required review is complete. An estimate is not a guarantee unless the product page expressly states otherwise.

  • Standard delivery timeframe: to be confirmed
  • Manual review requirements: to be confirmed
  • Support escalation timeframe: to be confirmed

3. Delivery method

Sensitive order information should be provided only through the approved protected customer workflow. Credentials must not appear in public URLs, analytics events, page source, or email subject lines.

4. Customer responsibilities

Customers must provide accurate order contact information, review the product requirements, and ensure their purchase and intended use comply with applicable law and platform rules.

5. Delivery issues

If an order appears delayed or incomplete, contact support using the confirmed order number. Never send passwords or unnecessary sensitive information through an unsecured support form.
