SMM & Digital Services Marketplace HTML Template

LinkSMM is a front-end template for digital agencies, social media teams, consultants, and service marketplaces. It uses semantic HTML, Bootstrap's grid and utilities, custom SCSS, and dependency-free JavaScript.

Front-end only: WordPress, WooCommerce, authentication, payments, email delivery, and databases are not included.

Getting started

Extract the ZIP and open index.html. A local server is recommended while editing: 

python3 -m http.server 4173

Customization

Brand

Replace assets/images/logo.svg and logo-white.svg. Keep equivalent dimensions or update their width and height attributes.

Colors and fonts

Core design tokens are at the top of style.scss under :root. Update primary, accent, text, background, radius, shadow, and font variables there.

Navigation

The menu uses WordPress-friendly menu-item-has-children and sub-menu classes through three levels. Preserve the nested ul > li > a + ul structure when editing.

Compile SCSS

npx sass assets/css/style.scss assets/css/style.css --style=expanded

JavaScript features

assets/js/custom.js controls the responsive menu and backdrop, submenu toggles, header search, back-to-top button, accordions, tabs, quantity controls, product gallery, blog filtering and search, and front-end form validation messages.

Interactive controls retain required ARIA state attributes. Decorative or redundant section relationships have been omitted to keep markup readable.

Forms and commerce

Forms validate in the browser but do not transmit data. Connect forms to your own PHP handler, API, CRM, or WordPress integration. Cart, checkout, login, registration, dashboard, and order confirmation are interface templates and require backend development.

Never collect passwords or payment information until the site is connected to an appropriately secured production system.

Images

Envato packaging: AI-generated product and blog images are for the hosted preview only. Do not include them in the downloadable ZIP. Replace them with redistribution-safe placeholders or properly licensed assets and disclose that preview images are not included.

Maintain the existing 1200 × 750 aspect ratio for product and blog images to avoid layout shifts.

Credits and licenses

The template uses Bootstrap 5.3.8, Bootstrap Icons, Inter, and Manrope. Full links and license notes are in ../licensing/credits-and-licenses.txt.

Support and production checklist

  • Replace logo, contact details, social links, testimonials, prices, and legal copy.
  • Connect forms, authentication, cart, and payment workflows.
  • Use only licensed downloadable assets.
  • Test the customized site in current Chrome, Firefox, Edge, and Safari.
  • Validate privacy, tax, refund, and service terms for the target business and jurisdiction.
