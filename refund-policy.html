Refund and Replacement Policy

Sample conditions for reviewing digital product issues, replacement eligibility, and support requests.

This sample policy requires legal review and final business rules before publication.
ScopeEligibilityNot eligibleRequest process

1. Scope

Digital products may have limited refund options after delivery. Any replacement or refund conditions must be clearly stated before checkout and applied consistently.

2. Potential eligibility

A request may be reviewed when the delivered product materially differs from the confirmed listing, required information was not delivered, or another published replacement condition applies.

The final reporting window is to be confirmed before launch.

3. Situations generally not eligible

  • Change of mind after digital delivery
  • Use outside the stated product conditions
  • Failure to follow published customer requirements
  • Requests submitted after the approved reporting window
  • Platform action or performance not expressly guaranteed by the listing

4. How to request a review

Contact support with the order number, a factual description of the issue, and only the evidence reasonably required to review the request. Do not send passwords through an unsecured form.

Outcome

After review, the final system may provide a replacement, refund, partial remedy, or explanation based on the published conditions and applicable law.
