# LinkSMM Upload Checklist

## Before upload

- [ ] Open and review every page in current Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge.
- [ ] Test desktop, tablet, and mobile breakpoints on physical devices where possible.
- [ ] Replace demo contact details, external links, prices, dates, and policy text as needed.
- [ ] Confirm all preview claims are accurate and remove unsupported statistics or ratings.
- [ ] Run `./build-themeforest-package.sh` from the project root.
- [ ] Extract and inspect `dist/linksmm-html-template-1.0.0.zip` in a clean directory.
- [ ] Confirm no preview-only AI images are included in the ZIP.
- [ ] Confirm the documentation opens and all packaged pages have working local assets.
- [ ] Upload `preview/cover.png` as the main preview image.
- [ ] Use the wide homepage, shop, and mobile-showcase images in the preview gallery.
- [ ] Paste and adapt `submission/item-description.md` in the marketplace listing editor.
- [ ] Choose the most accurate current ThemeForest category and attributes during submission.

## Final legal and support review

- [ ] Confirm redistribution rights for every file in the download package.
- [ ] Review Bootstrap and Bootstrap Icons notices in `licensing/credits-and-licenses.txt`.
- [ ] Define the support policy and response schedule shown on the item page.
- [ ] Keep preview-only assets separate from the customer download.
