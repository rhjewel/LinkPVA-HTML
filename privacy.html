Privacy Policy

Information about data collection, use, retention, customer choices, and service providers.

Data collectedHow data is usedSharingRetentionChoices

1. Information collected

The website may collect customer contact, billing, order, support, and technical information necessary to provide the service. Only information needed to provide and support the selected service should be collected.

Account passwords or unnecessary credentials should never be submitted through public contact forms.

2. How information is used

  • Process and deliver confirmed orders
  • Provide customer and project support
  • Maintain transaction and compliance records
  • Protect the website and prevent misuse
  • Improve the service where appropriate consent or another lawful basis exists

3. Service providers

Information may be shared only with approved providers needed for hosting, payment, email, analytics, security, or customer support. Providers should be documented in the production privacy policy.

4. Retention and security

Personal information should be kept only as long as required for the stated purpose, legal obligations, dispute handling, and security. Appropriate access controls, backups, and encryption should be implemented.

5. Customer choices

Depending on applicable law, customers may have rights to access, correct, delete, restrict, or object to certain processing. Customers can submit privacy requests through the published contact method.

Privacy contact

Submit privacy questions through the contact page. Privacy requests are reviewed through the published support channel.
